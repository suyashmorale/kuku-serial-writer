# The Husband Who Called at Dawn — 200-episode arc plan

**Premise:** In a sleepy hill town, a newlywed who devours cheap crime novels becomes the prime suspect when her husband's charred body is found — but the next morning, her phone rings with his voice.

**Brief:** Bollywood pulp-noir romantic thriller in the spirit of Haseen Dillruba, original story. Fictional Himachal hill town, Indian joint family, passionate suspicious marriage, jealous ex-lover, twisty family secrets, dark humour, occasional Hindi words.

**Logline:** In a Himachal hill town, newlywed Meher Sood is accused of burning her husband alive. At 6:12 the next morning, he calls her. His return clears up one lie and traps their joint family inside a far older one.

## Characters

- **Meher Sood, née Bedi, 29** (Newlywed, paperback obsessive and initial murder suspect): Keeps a pencil in the spine of whatever lurid novel she is reading. Came to Kesarghat expecting a difficult marriage, not a corpse wearing her husband's ring. *Arc:* From a bride defending herself in someone else's house to a partner who can leave, stay or tell her own story by choice.
- **Aadit Sood, 33** (Meher's husband; apparent victim and actual dawn caller): Runs the family's apple business with his elder brother. He is generous in public and evasive at the kitchen table. *Arc:* Returns alive by episode 8, but has to earn back the much harder belief that he will stay and speak plainly.
- **Tara Thakur, 32** (Aadit's former lover; Manu's sister): Runs Anardana Dhaba at the bus bend. She can tell a driver by the sound of his horn and still cannot bear the smell of hospital soap. *Arc:* Neither a romantic obstacle nor a saint: she gains a son, loses the fantasy of getting his first eight years back, and builds a relationship on the years ahead.
- **Shanta Sood, 61** (Matriarch of Cedar Terrace): Counts household sugar by the tin and remembers every guest's preferred pickle. Her acts of care are real; so is her capacity to control. *Arc:* Loses the power to define the family by herself and eventually tells the truth without bargaining for forgiveness.
- **Raghav Sood, 39** (Aadit's elder brother and Keshav's father): Handles the sorting shed, the creditors and Keshav's school shoes. He believes endurance entitles him to trust. *Arc:* His alibi breaks his marriage open; his decision to consent to Keshav's parentage test makes a different kind of fatherhood possible.
- **Kavya Sood, 36** (Raghav's wife, Keshav's mother and the killer): Mends school uniforms so neatly the repair becomes the strongest seam. She has raised Keshav since infancy. *Arc:* A formidable parent whose possessiveness leads to murder. Her confession is a choice to stop making Keshav carry her lies, not a bid for instant absolution.
- **Keshav 'Kesu' Sood, 8** (Raghav and Kavya's son; Tara and Aadit's biological child): Collects bus tickets and carves crooked birds from fallen walnut twigs. Knows which floorboard creaks outside his bedroom. *Arc:* Remains a child with games, loyalties and ordinary demands while the adults learn that knowing his origins does not require him to stop loving anyone.
- **Naina Sood, 25** (Youngest Sood sibling): Repairs customers' phones at a Lower Bazaar counter and is treated at home as though she still breaks them. *Arc:* Her silence initially protects her reputation; telling the family what Manu intended helps strip their excuses away.
- **Bhuvan Negi, 52** (Kesarghat police inspector; Tara and Manu's maternal uncle): Keeps peppermints in his uniform pocket and knows which local witnesses will refuse a second cup of tea. *Arc:* Starts the fire investigation, steps aside when Manu is identified, and remains a fallible member of the family the case has wounded.

## Threads

- **T01** The body in Aadit's ring (payoff ep 12): A coat and ring make identification seem certain. Prompt examination challenges it, forcing the family to acknowledge an unknown dead man.
- **T02** The call from the living husband (payoff ep 22): Meher verifies the dawn call, Aadit returns, and she eventually learns what he heard from Manu and why he left her exposed overnight.
- **T03** Give the dead man his name (payoff ep 42): A healed forearm injury and the relationships he left behind identify the victim as Manu Thakur. Tara must grieve a brother she had also distrusted.
- **T04** Naina and Manu (payoff ep 99): Their concealed affair explains why Manu could reach the shed unnoticed and why Naina resisted naming his last appointment.
- **T05** Meher's other name (payoff ep 111): Her anonymous crime writing supplies a persuasive accusation against her. She refuses an offer to sell a sensational account of the family and claims her work openly.
- **T06** The baby Tara buried (payoff ep 119): Tara first learns that her newborn lived. Finding him becomes urgent, but she cannot demand an answer from every child she meets.
- **T07** Bhuvan's old favor (payoff ep 138): Manu's uncle admits how he once helped him leave town and why Tara stopped believing either man's assurances.
- **T08** Whose son is Keshav? (payoff ep 143): Raghav consents to a parentage test after listening to the people who raised and lost the boy. Its answer changes Keshav's future without dictating it.
- **T09** The price of the orchard (payoff ep 156): Raghav wants to sell Bagh Number Seven to cover a failed sorting-machine investment; Aadit calls it surrender. They find a smaller business they can run without owning one another.
- **T10** Aadit and Tara's unfinished goodbye (payoff ep 159): Old desire complicates every meeting until they acknowledge that a shared child does not restore their former romance.
- **T11** Shanta's account of that winter (payoff ep 164): Contradictions in her story of the birth accumulate. She finally admits taking the baby and telling two parents he had died.
- **T12** The borrowed coat, ring and shawl (payoff ep 174): Meher traces who could have taken each object from Cedar Terrace. Their arrangement at the shed reveals deliberate staging after Manu's death.
- **T13** Raghav's alibi (payoff ep 181): His wish to protect Kavya survives even the proof about Keshav. He finally withdraws the alibi and says what he saw at the shed.
- **T14** Who killed Manu? (payoff ep 188): Suspicion passes through the people Manu loved, threatened and disappointed. Confronted with the cost of another lie to Keshav, Kavya admits the fatal blow and the fire.
- **T15** Kavya after the confession (payoff ep 193): Her account is checked against the hidden broken hook. Raghav and Tara reject both revenge through Keshav and the pretense that his mother did nothing wrong.
- **T16** A marriage without alibis (payoff ep 200): Meher and Aadit repeatedly choose honesty when silence would be easier. They end the series together on terms Meher can name, within a family Keshav is allowed to define for himself.

## Acts

- **Act 1 — The Wrong Husband** (eps 1-40): Clear Meher of the apparent murder while making Aadit's return a problem for their marriage, not a solution. *Turn:* At episode 40, Tara learns Aadit heard from Manu that their child might be alive and told Meher before telling her. She refuses to let Aadit speak for her again.
- **Act 2 — A Name for the Dead** (eps 41-80): Identify Manu, give his relationships weight, and move suspicion from the obvious romantic triangle into Cedar Terrace. *Turn:* At episode 80, Raghav catches Kavya using his forged signature to transfer Keshav's school place. He stops the transfer but keeps the alibi he gave her.
- **Act 3 — The Child Who Lived** (eps 81-120): Reverse the apparent motive for Manu's death: he meant to end a deception about a child, not prolong a profitable scandal. *Turn:* At episode 120, Meher admits she withheld a specific clue pointing to Keshav while seeking certainty. Tara ends their alliance: being treated carefully is not the same as being trusted.
- **Act 4 — No Surrender** (eps 121-160): Establish Keshav's parentage without treating a test as an answer to where he belongs; untangle the orchard and the two former lovers. *Turn:* At episode 160, Kavya tells Raghav she has known Keshav's parentage for two years. He realizes she let him defend a story of willing surrender she knew he had never verified.
- **Act 5 — What the Fire Could Not Take** (eps 161-200): Prove the abduction, staging and killing separately, then make the adults bear their consequences without asking Keshav to settle their claims. *Turn:* By episode 200, Kavya and Shanta face proceedings, Keshav has been told his history in words he can bear, and Meher and Aadit choose a marriage in which neither may keep a protective lie for the other.

### Arc 1: Six Twelve (eps 1-10)

*Find out whether the man calling Meher at dawn is Aadit.*

1. **Six Twelve** — With Aadit’s coat and ring found on a body in the burned shed, Meher becomes a suspect—until her phone rings at 6:12 and a man says he is Aadit.
2. **The Answer She Gives** — Meher gives Bhuvan her movements on the night of the fire, but her effort to sound certain leaves him with a time she cannot account for.
3. **The Driver’s Number** — Naina calls back the dawn number and reaches a bus driver, replacing a possible hoax with a person who can be questioned.
4. **The Driver's View** — Jitendra confirms that the caller asked for Meher by name, but cannot identify him. During Tara's questioning of the driver, a brief memory of Manu at the bus bend lets the audience meet her brother without connecting him to the burned body. _(plan v2)_
5. **What the Ring Proves** — Bhuvan separates the ring and coat from the body wearing them, so Meher gains a reason to demand examination but no reason yet to declare Aadit alive.
6. **Tara’s Cup** — Tara refuses to confirm the caller from Meher’s description and tells her instead what it cost to believe the family once before, when she was told her newborn had died.
7. **The Last Bus Out** — Meher and Naina follow Jitendra’s route and learn the caller did not board his bus, narrowing where Aadit could be without finding him.
8. **At the Gate** — Aadit walks into Cedar Terrace alive, ending the doubt about the caller while making his absence during Meher’s questioning impossible to ignore.
9. **The Paperback** — While Bhuvan takes Aadit’s account, Meher hides the crime paperback she published under a pen name, adding a fresh secret to a marriage already strained by his silence.
10. **The Tin** — At the Sood dinner table, the family's argument over the unidentified body and Aadit's night away forces Meher to ask who had access to his coat and ring. With Bhuvan present but asking fewer questions, Aadit says he left his ring in a tin at Cedar Terrace before going to the shed; Meher has never been shown it. _(plan v2)_

### Arc 2: Not His Bones (eps 11-20)

*Force a proper examination of the body and keep suspicion from falling back on Meher by default.*

11. **The Tin on the Shelf** — Meher finds Aadit's ring tin at Cedar Terrace as the family disputes who used the kitchen before the fire. Their conflicting recollections leave no reliable account of who opened it, and the tin is preserved for Bhuvan to inspect. _(plan v2)_
12. **The Wearer's Bones** — The examination rules out Aadit as the burned man, leaving the coat and ring as evidence of clothing rather than identity.
13. **A Sleeve in the Ash** — When Bhuvan asks how Meher recognized the burned coat, her precise answer helps him but gives him another reason to test her account.
14. **Two Death Stories** — Tara asks Shanta for the date her baby died and receives a different sequence of events from the one Shanta gave her years ago.
15. **The Records Window** — Bhuvan's request for records of healed forearm injuries stalls at a closed clinic, while Naina finds a lawful way to preserve the request until morning.
16. **An Old Break** — A clinic entry confirms the injury could have healed years before the fire, but its incomplete index cannot yet identify the burned man.
17. **The Picnic Slip** — Keshav hears adults argue about Tara's dead baby, mistakes their lowered voices for talk of replacing his mother, and refuses to hand Kavya his school picnic slip.
18. **Both Names** — Kavya promises Keshav she will attend the picnic, then presses Shanta to stop speaking about Tara's baby where he can hear.
19. **At the Bus Bend** — Meher and Bhuvan check whether anyone saw the unknown man arrive before the fire, separating a witness's actual sighting from what she learned afterward.
20. **The Pickle Jar** — Pressed to choose between her two accounts of the baby's death, Shanta corrects Meher's pickle recipe instead, and Aadit recognizes that she is evading the question.

### Arc 3: The Orchard Ledger (eps 21-30)

*Pin down Aadit's movements before the fire and the cost of keeping the orchard.*

21. **The Tin and the Time** — Meher gets Aadit to put a time to his visit to the shed, but he stops his account just before explaining why he stayed away.
22. **The Night He Kept** — Aadit tells Meher he met Manu at the shed, heard their child might be alive, and hid overnight while she answered questions about his apparent death.
23. **The Receipt** — Naina helps check a repair-counter time against Aadit's account, then pockets a receipt bearing a number she recognizes. The number recalls a specific exchange with Manu from their relationship, showing his charm and a way he let her down without explaining what happened at the shed. _(plan v2)_
24. **A Price for Number Seven** — Raghav shows Aadit the sorting-machine payments and can no longer pretend the orchard will cover the next one.
25. **Home All Evening** — Asked about the fire night, Raghav says Kavya was with him at Cedar Terrace, committing himself to an alibi she did not ask him to give aloud.
26. **The Forearm List** — Meher brings Bhuvan a possible name for the dead man, but the injury records cannot yet establish whose body was in the shed.
27. **Counterfoil** — Naina retrieves the repair receipt before Meher can copy it, but learns the clerk remembers more than a customer number.
28. **Two Columns** — Meher separates what Aadit can verify about the shed from what he merely feared Manu meant, and refuses to turn either column into an excuse for his silence.
29. **Before the Inventory** — Raghav tries to raise the overdue payment by proposing a quick sale of Bagh Number Seven, but Aadit demands the machine's original invoice first.
30. **What Else** — Meher gives Bhuvan the shed times she can support, then confronts Aadit with the fact that every useful answer has come only after she found its edge herself.

### Arc 4: What Tara Wasn't Told (eps 31-40)

*Locate Manu's last movements without turning Tara into an exhibit in Meher and Aadit's marriage.*

31. **The Bend at Seven** — A bus-bend tea seller places the unidentified man on the road to the drying shed before the fire, giving Bhuvan a witness to interview rather than another family guess.
32. **A Page in the Bazaar** — A passage from Meher's fire-story paperback circulates through Lower Bazaar, and she chooses not to invent a denial when a shopkeeper asks why its shed sounds familiar.
33. **The Last Bus Down** — Tara catches a departing bus to question its conductor, but his account rules out the easy claim that the unknown man left town before the shed fire.
34. **The Crooked Sleeve** — Asked about old injuries among missing men, Tara recalls that Manu always rolled up one sleeve because a healed forearm break made the cuff catch.
35. **Two Picnic Plates** — Keshav asks Kavya to pack enough food for both his parents at the school picnic, then hides a ticket from Tara's dhaba when Kavya checks his bag.
36. **The Receipt Drawer** — Shanta produces a receipt for Manu's forearm treatment but tries to keep Tara from taking it to Bhuvan, turning a household errand into an argument over who may ask about her brother.
37. **Four Minutes to the Shed** — Meher and Aadit walk the route from the bus bend while Bhuvan checks the conductor's ticket roll, and their timings leave room for Aadit's meeting with the unknown man but not for his account to stand without scrutiny.
38. **A Record, Not a Name** — Bhuvan sends the old treatment details for comparison with the body's forearm injury, while Tara refuses Aadit's offer to deliver whatever answer comes back.
39. **Before the Fire Story** — Meher shows Bhuvan that her paperback predates the shed fire, then notices Aadit still answers Tara's questions about his meeting at the shed without saying what the man told him.
40. **At the Walnut Steps** — Tara learns that Aadit told Meher about the man's hint that their child might be alive before telling her, and she rejects his attempt to choose when she should hear it.

### Arc 5: Manu Thakur (eps 41-50)

*Give the dead man a defensible identification and his sister a truthful account.*

41. **The Clinic Name** — Tara gives Bhuvan the name of the clinic that treated Manu's broken forearm. As he records it, Bhuvan recalls the money he once gave Manu to leave town and the lie he told Tara; the clinic remains a lead to check, not proof of the body's identity. _(plan v2)_
42. **His Name** — Comparison with Manu's medical record identifies the burned man, ending the question of who died and making Bhuvan's family connection impossible to set aside.
43. **A Different Inspector** — Bhuvan withdraws from the investigation and Inspector Leela Bisht takes charge, leaving Tara to insist that being family must not mean receiving filtered answers.
44. **Property Receipt** — Tara claims Manu's released belongings, but the inventory forces her to confront how little she knows about where he spent his last weeks.
45. **Two Sugars** — Naina knows exactly how Manu took his tea but calls every meeting with him repair-counter business, giving Tara a reason to doubt her careful answers.
46. **Paid Through the Month** — A rent receipt in Manu's belongings shows he paid for his room well beyond the night he died, undercutting the story that he planned simply to disappear.
47. **No Family Seal** — Aadit brings Bisht his account of the shed meeting, and she checks his timing without letting either him or Meher handle evidence on the family's behalf.
48. **What She Could Name** — Naina offers to help Tara sort Manu's things, but her precise memories and narrow account of their acquaintance make Tara send her away.
49. **Before Ten** — With the landlord due at ten, Tara arranges access to Manu's room through Bisht and asks Meher to come as company, not as an investigator.
50. **Addressed to Shanta** — Tara stops Manu's papers from being cleared and finds proof that he began returning money he had taken, with a repayment addressed to Shanta the day before he died.

### Arc 6: A Brother's Bad Habits (eps 51-60)

*Learn why Manu took Shanta's money without cleaning up the man he was.*

51. **What Manu Took** — Tara asks Bhuvan for the names of people Manu stole from, and their argument leaves her with a list he will not help her check.
52. **Not a Police Seal** — Meher brings Manu's repayment note to Bisht, who agrees to check its origin but refuses to let Meher collect evidence in the police's name.
53. **The Counter Book** — Tara and Meher reach the shop before its books are packed away, but leave the relevant entry for Bisht to obtain properly.
54. **The Second Envelope** — Bisht establishes that Manu sent a second repayment toward Shanta, changing the first note from an isolated gesture into a deliberate attempt to return her money.
55. **His Usual Tea** — Naina keeps a memory of Manu cheating her over a cup of tea to herself rather than offer Tara a tidier story of a repentant brother.
56. **Without the Badge** — Bhuvan offers to question one of Manu's old creditors, but Tara insists that he either visit as her uncle or give the name to Bisht.
57. **A Paid Delay** — The creditor's account confirms Manu took money to put off difficult conversations, giving Tara a reason to suspect Shanta paid for time rather than a service.
58. **What She Bought** — Pressed by Tara, Shanta admits paying Manu to postpone telling her something, but will not name it or say how long she expected him to wait.
59. **One Fare Home** — Keshav visits Tara's dhaba for a snack and keeps his bus ticket, while Tara lets an ordinary visit stay ordinary despite everything she has learned about Shanta.
60. **Transfer Dates** — Kavya finds Keshav's saved dhaba ticket and returns it to his schoolbook, but begins considering a move he has never asked for.

### Arc 7: The Impounded Sorter (eps 61-70)

*Recover an accurate account of Raghav's debt before he sells Bagh Number Seven.*

61. **The Inventory Notice** — A lender's notice gives Raghav four days to account for the impounded sorter, turning his talk of selling Bagh Number Seven into an immediate choice.
62. **A Key Is Not an Answer** — Bisht checks who could enter Cedar Terrace, but Shanta's tidy account of the coat cupboard leaves the ring tin and Meher's shawl unexplained.
63. **Supervised Access** — Aadit secures the lender's permission to inspect the sorter, and Naina gets its failing display working long enough to show that the purchase figures are still stored there.
64. **Two Prices** — Meher copies the sorter's entries against the lender's statement and finds that Raghav has counted money already owed as cash available to save the orchard.
65. **The Picnic Slip** — Keshav asks Raghav to sign a school picnic slip while Kavya presses for a quick orchard sale, and Raghav promises the child a normal school week he cannot yet secure.
66. **The Sale Price** — Raghav brings a buyer's offer for Bagh Number Seven to Aadit, who refuses to sign until they can compare it with the copied accounts.
67. **Before the Seal** — During the lender's final inspection, Naina retrieves a missing page of machine totals under the clerk's eye, giving Meher enough figures to check the proposed sale.
68. **Room to Wait** — The completed figures show Raghav cannot cover what he promised, but can meet the immediate demand without selling Bagh Number Seven this week.
69. **What Was in the House** — Bisht separately checks the family's accounts of the coat, ring and shawl, while Tara declines to turn her memory of the night into a guess about the thief.
70. **The Loss in Rupees** — With the sale deferred, Raghav tells Aadit exactly how much value the sorter lost and finds he cannot explain why he kept that loss from his brother.

### Arc 8: The Transfer Form (eps 71-80)

*Stop the family's fear from becoming a decision made over Keshav's head.*

71. **Two Signatures** — Keshav brings home a picnic permission slip and makes Raghav and Kavya promise to attend, turning their talk of a school move into a promise they must either keep or break.
72. **Tea at Cedar Terrace** — Shanta invites Tara to discuss her dead baby at Cedar Terrace, but Tara refuses to let the Sood household set the terms of that conversation.
73. **At the Bus Bend** — Kavya goes to collect Keshav after spotting him near Tara's dhaba, but finds he has gone there to ask about the picnic bus, not his past.
74. **A Place at the Table** — Raghav tries to settle a household argument over Keshav's visits to the bus bend, but Keshav asks why everyone speaks softly whenever he comes in.
75. **The School Office** — Kavya asks a school clerk when transfers close and learns she can submit a request before finding Keshav a new place.
76. **No New School** — Raghav rejects a transfer after Kavya presents it as a precaution, forcing her to choose between his refusal and the plan she has begun.
77. **The Picnic Bus** — Raghav and Kavya both come to Keshav's picnic, but Kavya's watchfulness spoils his chance to show them the walnut birds he has been talking about for weeks.
78. **Before Closing** — While Raghav checks the orchard accounts with Aadit, Kavya submits the transfer request despite knowing Raghav has refused it.
79. **A Call from School** — A routine call about Keshav's records alerts Raghav to a transfer request he never made, and he asks the clerk to hold everything until he sees it.
80. **Whom Are We Running From?** — Raghav sees his forged signature and withdraws Kavya's transfer request, but stops short of withdrawing the alibi he gave her for the night of Manu's death.

### Arc 9: Last Appointments (eps 81-90)

*Reconstruct Manu's final day from people rather than a supposed master document.*

81. **The Picnic List** — While Keshav checks that both his parents are still coming to the picnic, Tara agrees to help Meher reconstruct Manu’s final day—but only from witnesses she can question herself.
82. **A Reason to Strike** — Aadit gives Inspector Leela Bisht a precise account of his shed meeting and hears how readily his anger over the child can be mistaken for a reason to kill Manu.
83. **Before the Bus Pulls Out** — Meher and Tara catch a departing bus conductor who remembers serving Manu at the bend, placing him there later than Aadit’s first estimate allowed.
84. **One Trip Too Many** — Bisht compares Dev’s account with the dhaba’s tea orders and finds that Manu returned to the bus bend after an errand, defeating the family’s neat picture of one straight walk to the shed.
85. **The Address in Pencil** — Bhuvan gives Tara the address of a room Manu once rented, but refuses to use his former police access to find its occupants for her.
86. **After Closing** — Naina insists she met Manu by chance as she shut the repair counter, then resists Bisht’s request for a closing time.
87. **Five Fifteen** — Before the neighboring stall shuts, Meher and Bisht reach its owner and establish that Naina’s counter was locked well before the chance meeting she described.
88. **Not a Chance Meeting** — Confronted with the five-fifteen closing time, Naina admits she misstated when she saw Manu but still calls their conversation repair business.
89. **The Room Behind the Bazaar** — Tara visits Manu’s former landlady and learns he had tried to repay an old debt without asking the woman to forgive how he incurred it.
90. **Six Thirty-Five** — After Bisht establishes the time of Manu’s last appointment from a separate witness, Naina names that time before Bisht tells her where the meeting took place.

### Arc 10: The Warning (eps 91-100)

*Make Naina state what Manu intended and whom she sent him to see.*

91. **Closing Time** — When Bisht asks how Naina knew the time of Manu’s last appointment, Naina replaces her chance-meeting story with a new one that still does not fit the repair counter’s hours.
92. **The Alibi at Home** — Raghav tries to keep his account of Kavya’s evening separate from the new questions about Manu, but Kavya asks him to repeat it word for word.
93. **The Last Bus** — Tara catches the bus-bend conductor before his shift ends and learns that Manu changed his route on the day he died.
94. **Money Back** — Tara finds that Manu had set aside another repayment, changing her picture of his final week without making his old thefts disappear.
95. **Two Cups** — Naina quietly removes a second tea cup from her repair-counter shelf, then finds she cannot give Tara a truthful memory of Manu without explaining why it is hers.
96. **One More Minute** — Bisht checks Raghav’s alibi against the bus-bend timing, and Raghav holds to it even after realizing it leaves a gap he cannot account for.
97. **Before the Shutter Falls** — Meher and Bisht reach the repair counter before its records are cleared for a new month and recover a dated service slip that places Manu there before his final trip.
98. **What the Numbers Say** — The call records contradict Naina’s revised story, and she agrees to give Bisht a full statement rather than make Tara hear another account built around omissions.
99. **Not Repair Work** — Naina admits that Manu was her lover and that she arranged his meeting with Kavya, turning her supposed chance encounters into deliberate concealment.
100. **The Warning** — Naina admits she warned Kavya that Manu had evidence Tara’s newborn survived and intended to tell Tara the next morning; told at once, Tara turns from the adults’ betrayals to finding the child.

### Arc 11: A Date on a Form (eps 101-110)

*Look for the living child without making Keshav, or any other child, submit to Tara's questions.*

101. **The Morning After** — Tara turns Naina’s admission into a search for records, rather than a confrontation with the children at Cedar Terrace.
102. **The Clinic Window** — A clinic clerk refuses Tara access to another patient’s records, so Inspector Leela Bisht begins a documented request instead.
103. **Before Four** — Tara and Bisht get the request to the records office before closing, but the register points them to a second, separately kept file.
104. **No Certificate Number** — Bisht confirms that a blank register field is not proof the baby lived, changing Tara’s promising discovery back into a question.
105. **Picnic Shoes** — Keshav’s worry about his school picnic brings Raghav and Kavya back to a practical promise the adults can actually keep.
106. **Who Signed?** — Pressed for the surrendering parent’s name, Shanta repeats that Raghav and Kavya received a surrendered infant but cannot identify anyone who consented.
107. **At the Gate** — Aadit arrives at Cedar Terrace ready to demand an answer from Shanta, but Tara stops him and chooses to give Bisht her own statement.
108. **The School Office** — A routine picnic query leads Raghav to Keshav’s school file, but he declines Meher’s suggestion that the family pass its contents around as evidence.
109. **A Seat on the Bus** — Tara keeps a planned bus trip with Keshav ordinary, while Bhuvan accepts that helping her means answering only what she asks.
110. **A Date on a Form** — While Raghav puts away Keshav’s school papers, Meher notices that a date in his file falls uncomfortably close to the day Tara was told her baby died.

### Arc 12: Under Her Own Name (eps 111-120)

*Let Meher tell the truth about her work and let Tara pursue the child as a person, not a piece of proof.*

111. **The Name on the Cover** — Meher publicly claims the crime paperback she wrote under a pen name and refuses a Lower Bazaar bookseller’s offer to pay for a Sood-family exposé.
112. **One Date Apart** — Meher compares the school-file date with the date on Tara’s own clinic papers and finds them close enough to trouble her, but not close enough to prove anything.
113. **The Empty Column** — Bisht’s check of the available clinic entry confirms that a newborn left alive, but its destination is blank, defeating Meher’s hope of finding a conclusive record before she speaks.
114. **Picnic Morning** — When a late bus threatens Keshav’s school picnic, Kavya and Raghav get him there while Tara keeps the dhaba open instead of using the disruption to question him.
115. **What She Can Ask** — Tara gives Bisht the records she is entitled to share and asks for lawful checks, rather than seeking access to Keshav’s private file.
116. **Not Your Errand** — Aadit offers to ask Shanta about the dates, but Tara stops him, and Meher lets the conversation end without disclosing what she saw in the school file.
117. **The Birthday List** — While helping at a school event, Tara hears Keshav give his birthday for a public birthday list and recognizes the date as falling in the week her baby was born.
118. **The Walnut Birds** — Tara chooses not to follow Keshav home and instead asks Bhuvan to help her lay out what she knows, separating witnessed dates from what she desperately hopes they mean.
119. **Probably Him** — With the verified clinic timeline, Keshav’s publicly given birthday and Shanta’s inability to name a surrendering parent, Tara concludes that Keshav is probably her son, though she cannot yet establish parentage.
